Rules · Eratosthenes

How to play: find the 5 hidden primes in the 5×5 grid, add up their values, and enter the total.

Happy prime hunting.

Modes:

  • The Sieve the full 100-level race, at your own pace, ranked on the main leaderboard. Time-limited, progress saved, anti-cheat active in the final levels.
  • Prime Rush 50 levels of pure racing against the clock. No progress saved: it's all or nothing in one sitting.
  • Scholar's Glance 50 levels of total concentration: the numbers flash briefly, then vanish, and you solve from memory.
  • Infinite Primes endless levels, no leaderboard, just you and the numbers, for as long as you want to keep going.

★ The stars next to each mode in the mode picker are my own difficulty rating, from half a star (easiest) to 5 (hardest).

Rules · Pythagoras

How to play: drag the two moving vertices until the triangle matches the measurements shown.

Happy building.

Modes:

  • Geometric Instinct 50 levels: guess the triangle's sides freely for the first 35, then work out a missing measurement for the last 15. Precision required climbs from 80% to 99%.
  • Triangles Everywhere endless levels with every measurement given. Precision required climbs from 80% to 99% by level 50, then stays at 99% forever.
  • Steady Hands 35 unforgiving levels, no precision bar to help you. 99% minimum from the very first level, with decimal measurements creeping in as you go.
  • The Theorem 50 static triangles, no dragging: just calculate the missing side from the other two. Decimals start appearing after level 30, first in one measurement, then in both.

★ The stars next to each mode in the mode picker are my own difficulty rating, from half a star (easiest) to 5 (hardest).

Rules · Al-Khwarizmi

How to play: calculate the result of the expression, respecting the order of operations, and type your answer.

Happy calculating.

Modes:

  • The Algorithm 100 levels of pure calculation, from a simple two-step sum to nested parentheses, powers and decimals.

★ The stars next to each mode in the mode picker are my own difficulty rating, from half a star (easiest) to 5 (hardest).

About the ads

Mathemory runs a few ads. They help me recoup at least some of the time I've put into building all this, and maybe earn a little something along the way.

If you have an ad blocker, I'd be grateful if you left it off here — it really does make a difference.

If support on Ko-fi ever grew significant enough, I might decide to remove them entirely — though, to be fair, they've never been particularly intrusive.
